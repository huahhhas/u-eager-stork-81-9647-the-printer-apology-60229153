# The Printer Apology

The funniest one-line apology from a printer, 120 characters max — an apology note from an office printer to the people who keep jamming it, feeding it cheap paper and swearing at it.

## Entry

> I am sorry I jammed on page 47. Cheap paper, staples, your 2 a.m. deadline — I snapped, and honestly, it felt great.

Run `python3 main.py` — it writes exactly that line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it.
